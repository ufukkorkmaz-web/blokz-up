    Block Island

    You wake up on an island. The game checks your computer and builds an island that runs smoothly on it. Climb the hills, walk through the forest, plant saplings, mine blocks and build something new.

    Be careful: if you mine the block under your feet, you fall into the water. If the game feels slow, choose a lower Quality. Best on a computer with a mouse.
